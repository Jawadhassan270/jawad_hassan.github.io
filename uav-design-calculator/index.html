Mission to preliminary design

Design workflow

1. Mission requirements

Set payload, flight distance, endurance, operating altitude and flight speeds.

2. Mass + wing

Estimate MTOW and derive required wing area, span, chord, lift and drag.

3. Fuselage + tail

Choose tail-volume coefficients and fuselage ratios from starting-point reference tables.

4. Propulsion + battery

Use propulsive efficiency to estimate required electrical power, add mission phases and select real components.

5. Validate and iterate

Check CG, stability, airfoil data, propulsion bench testing, mass budget and structural strength externally.

VLM2 cruise & stall analysis

Spanwise lift distribution

Blue: local cₗ at cruise · dashed: airfoil cₗ,max near stall. Spanwise values exclude nonlinear separation and control deflections.

The values are approximate stored section cₗ,max summaries (Ncrit 9), not experimental whole-wing results. The section stall limit is imposed externally; inviscid VLM2 cannot resolve stall or viscous/profile drag. Changes in taper, twist, sweep, Reynolds number, surface roughness and stall progression can alter wing CL,max. Verify using XFOIL polars and finite-wing analysis (e.g., XFLR5 or CFD) before flight.
